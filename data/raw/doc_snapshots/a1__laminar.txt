# Laminar

Laminar is an [**open-source**](https://github.com/lmnr-ai/lmnr) observability platform for AI agents that proactively analyzes every trace and alerts you when a run goes wrong. Our fine-tuned model [**flow-1**](https://laminar.sh/blog/flow-1) delivers frontier-level trace analysis at a cost that makes it practical to monitor every run without sampling.

These findings help you turn real failures into eval cases, discover unknown failure modes, and prioritize improvements based on where users struggle.

Get started with Laminar Cloud or [self-host](https://laminar.sh/docs/self-hosting/overview.md) Laminar on your own infrastructure.

![Traces stream from your agent in production into flow-1, which reads every one of them; the count of traces read goes up with each. Most traces have no issue. Failing ones turn orange and land in Issues: Wrong refund amount grows from 3 to 5, Report contradicts tests from 1 to 2, and a new issue, Stuck retrying login, is discovered. The top issue then goes round the improvement loop: its traces are turned into eval cases, the fix is shipped to the agent, and the issue is marked fixed.](https://mintcdn.com/laminarai/epigMNHnrHWhbnrC/images/overview-every-trace.svg?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=epigMNHnrHWhbnrC\&q=85\&s=85dd7a59212bd7ba264d4f97cd1c4e83)

flow-1 reads every trace your agent produces. Failures collect into issues, including ones you didn't know about, and the top issue goes back to the agent through eval cases and a fix.
